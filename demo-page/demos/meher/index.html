03 — The Atelier

Nineteen days,
four hands.

Machines are quicker. They are not better. Our karigars have been with the house an average of twenty-two years — the youngest is Meher's own apprentice.

BIS 916IGI CertifiedConflict-free
  1. i

    The drawing

    Graphite on gridded vellum. No CAD until the client has held the paper.

  2. ii

    The pour

    22K alloyed in-house, cast in a lost-wax mould that is broken after one use.

  3. iii

    The setting

    Every stone seated by hand under a loupe. A bridal set takes eleven days here alone.

  4. iv

    The signature

    Polished, weighed, hallmarked, and struck with the setter's initials inside the band.

04 — Bespoke

Bring us the idea.
